Executive summary

One system of record for every programme stage — and every euro of it.

Acceleration programmes are run on goodwill and spreadsheets: applications lost in inboxes, scores in side files, stipends paid from memory, repayments reconciled by hand at quarter-end. finnspark replaces that with a governed pipeline: public application forms, an evaluator-scored selection board, cohort and learning management, a dual-currency disbursement engine for grants and repayable loans, a reconciled loan book — and an institutional reporting pack your board and donors can rely on. Every workflow is permission-guarded, auditable and bilingual, built for teams that answer to investment committees, not just to calendars.

Platform capabilities

Nine pillars, zero spreadsheets.

Form builders & public intake

Branded public apply pages plus a full builder suite — application forms with 10 field types, weighted scoring forms and investment forms, all multilingual. Applications never touch an inbox again.

Selection board

Configurable stages — Application → Evaluation → Pitching → Selection — with statuses, filters and weighted evaluator scoring averaged per applicant, then one-click invite-to-register.

Cohort & programme management

Programmes, cohorts and enrolled businesses with graduation tracking, mentor assignment, course-progress visibility and Excel exports — the operating picture of every cohort.

Learning management

The SME Business Manual as 13 structured courses — modules, lessons, video, file and quiz blocks — with per-founder progress tied to graduation criteria.

Disbursement engine

Track I grants (stipends, prototype vouchers) and Track II loans with co-financing grant tranches. Batch payments with per-item FX (EUR base / JOD payout), exact dates, automatic narratives, confirm/reopen governance.

Loan book & repayments

Import the loan book, record repayments in batches, reconcile every instalment against bank extracts. Principal vs interest, dual-currency balances, recovery rates per cohort.

Dealflow, committees & portfolio

Investment cases across stages, tiers and rounds; multi-level committee decisions; portfolio financials with loan/grant amounts, disbursed-to-date and payment schedules.

Document library

A branch-, programme- and business-scoped repository for onboarding packs, signed agreements and programme documents — controlled, findable, always current.

Collaboration & governance

Announcements with reactions, shared calendar, team chat and a notifications bell — on top of granular RBAC enforced on every endpoint and an immutable audit trail.

The lifecycle

Closed-loop capital governance, stage by stage.

01Apply

Founders apply through branded public forms — every field structured, bilingual, scored.

02Select

Evaluator scoring feeds committee-grade pipeline decisions. Winners become businesses.

03Onboard & learn

Cohorts onboard with mentors, document packs and the SME Business Manual.

04Disburse

Track I stipends and vouchers, Track II loans and co-financing grants — batched, FX'd, narrated.

05Recover

Repayment instalments land in batches, verified against bank extracts, principal tracked.

06Report

The board sees one reconciled picture — per cohort, per year, per currency.

Every stage feeds the next: applications become businesses, businesses receive structured finance, repayments flow back into the ledger — and the money story stays reconciled from the first application form to the last repaid euro.
Reporting

Institutional reporting, on demand.

Four dedicated dashboards — Businesses/Projects, Investment, Learning and Team — plus a programme funnel with cohort/year filtering that cascades across every tile and chart, and drill-down tables behind every number.

Portfolio snapshot

Portfolio companies, total invested, average ticket, invested by industry — the 10-second view for a board meeting.

Payments schedule & aging

Every scheduled instalment, paid vs due, and outstanding balances by aging bucket — recovery risk before it becomes a surprise.

Detailed information pack

11 sections — financial timeline, investment metrics, collateral, co-financing summary, CEO profile, sustainability, equity and more — paginated with one-click Excel export.
